# AETHER // Neon Promises — Changelog

- Includes Chapters 1–4 and a short branching epilogue.
- Expanded the story with distinct short-term outcomes for exploration, commitment and uncertainty/distance.
- Earlier dialogue decisions now create more visible follow-up lines and rewards.
- Newly unlocked gallery memories receive in-chat notices and previews.
- Updated message-scroll positioning to show the beginning of new conversations.
- Made exact stat-reward numbers optional and hidden by default.
- Rebalanced stat progression, especially Love and Trust.
- Added more MC internal perspective via Notes/journal entries.
- Expanded cast, relationship values, group chat and VEILROOM story elements.
- Retained local save export/import with earlier-save migration support.

This remains an alpha: many early scenes share a common route and not all planned branches or images are available. Please back up prior saves before upgrading.
